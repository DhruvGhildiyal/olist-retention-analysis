
# Stakeholder Brief

**To:** Data Analyst
**From:** Head of Operations, Olist
**Date:** 02-09-26
**Deadline:** 09-09-26
**Re:** Falling repeat purchase rate — cause and recommended action

---

## Context

Our repeat purchase rate has been sliding and the leadership team is arguing
about the cause. Marketing insists lead quality is unchanged and points at
fulfilment. Our seller operations lead says delivery performance is "within
normal range." Nobody has actually checked the data.

I need this settled with evidence, not opinion, before the next board review.

## What I need answered

1. **Is the decline real, and what is driving it?**
   Break the revenue movement down into its parts — are we acquiring fewer
   customers, are existing customers ordering less often, or are baskets
   getting smaller? I want to know which lever moved.

2. **Is delivery performance a factor, and how concentrated is the problem?**
   If late deliveries are hurting retention, quantify how much of the
   repeat-rate gap they explain. Then tell me whether this is a company-wide
   issue or a small number of sellers dragging everyone down. The answer
   changes what we do.

3. **What do I do on Monday, and what is it worth?**
   Give me a specific action with an estimated impact. "Improve delivery" is
   not an action.

## Deliverables

- A **two-page memo** I can forward to the CEO without editing. Lead with the
  recommendation, not the methodology.
- A **dashboard** my ops managers can check weekly — one executive view, one
  drill-down for investigating specific sellers or regions.

## Constraints and expectations

- Use only the internal order, delivery, review and customer data we have.
  We have no marketing spend or web analytics data for this period.
- Be explicit about what your analysis does **not** prove. I would rather know
  the limits than get a confident answer that falls apart in the boardroom.
  If the data can only show correlation, say so.
- If you find a data quality problem that changes the answer, flag it in the
  memo rather than quietly working around it.
